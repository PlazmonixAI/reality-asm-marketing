## Reality ASM (students and curious people)
An advanced simulation machine for physics, chemistry and maths. You ask a question or open a simulation, and a real
engine computes the answer (numpy, scipy, sympy). Every number on screen comes from the engine, not from guesses.
- About 180 engine tools and more than 90 interactive simulations.
- **Universe Map:** from the Solar System to the observable universe, with real star and galaxy catalogues, true sizes
  and distances when you zoom in, a Journey mode from the Big Bang to Earth, and sky events (meteor showers, comets,
  asteroid close approaches).
- **Space Program:** design rockets (PSLV and others ready-made), launch them, put satellites in orbit, Mission Control.
- **Labs:** Physics Lab (build your own experiments), Chemistry Lab bench, Maths Sandbox, Isotope Lab (build any atom
  from protons, neutrons and electrons; 2,939 real nuclides), Particle Collider (LHC-style ring and detector), Fusion
  Reactor (ITER-style tokamak), Fission Reactor, Quantum Universe (3D orbitals).
- **Engines and flight:** car engine, jet engine, rocket engines, airflow over a wing, forces of flight.
- **AI analyst** on every simulation: explains the result with units, assumptions and limits.
- Biology is not part of Reality ASM.

## ASM Teach (schools)
A separate product for classrooms and smart boards, at realityasm.com/teach. Schools sign up; the school admin adds teachers.
- 384 curriculum experiments for Classes 9 to 12: Science (physics, chemistry, maths), Commerce and Arts (Classes 11 and 12).
- Follows the school's board: CBSE (NCERT books) or ICSE/ISC.
- Smart whiteboard: write an equation by hand and it becomes a live graph; sketch a cube and it becomes 3D.
- AI co-teacher, teacher's own files (PDF, slides, videos) on the board.
- **Classes:** syllabus tracker per class (planned, taught, coverage %), tests made by the engine with worked solutions,
  homework links students open on a phone without an account, results per student and per question, quiz mode on the
  board, printable paper with answer key.
- School admin panel: teachers, usage, every class's syllabus coverage and test scores.
- Price: ₹22,500 a month for 30 boards (+ GST); ₹750 per extra board.
