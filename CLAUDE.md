# CLAUDE.md: Reality ASM marketing

This repo is for Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and X content for **Reality ASM** and **ASM Teach** (by Plazmonix AI).
The product code lives in `PlazmonixAI/reality-ai`; do not change the product from here.

Read `BRAND.md` before writing anything. The content skills are in `.claude/skills/` (from Ootto-AI/claude-content-skills, MIT).

## Rules for every post
- Instagram: exactly **5 hashtags**. Default set: `#RealityASM #PlazmonixAI #ScienceEducation #EdTech #MadeInIndia`.
- No image credits in posts or captions. Credits live in the website's terms and conditions.
- Copy reads like a person wrote it: no em dashes, no emojis in on-screen text, no hype words ("revolutionary",
  "game-changing", "unleash", "supercharge"). Say what the product does and who it helps.
- Never invent numbers, features, customers or testimonials. Use only what is in `BRAND.md`. If unsure, ask.
- Always write "Reality ASM" (not RealityASM or Reality-ASM).
- Reel design: black frame, three-line headline above a rounded video card, phonk audio. Each video gets its own
  structure; no rolling word lists; no plain list of labs.

## Posting and accounts
Claude cannot log into Instagram here. Write the hooks, scripts, captions, calendars and videos; the owner posts them.
Do not automate DMs or comments. The `comment-responder` and `agent-reach` skills: draft replies only, and ask before
installing any extra tool that `agent-reach` mentions.

## Folders
- `posts/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>/`: one folder per post (caption.md, script, cover text, final video or images).
- `videos/`: finished videos and source clips.
